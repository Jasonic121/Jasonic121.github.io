# The Beginning 🌟

My decision to join CMU was driven by its stellar reputation in computer science and engineering. Despite initial concerns about tuition and future uncertainties, this choice proved to be transformative. I set several ambitious goals for myself, which shaped my academic journey and personal growth throughout the program.
